Help & Frequently Asked Questions
Quick FAQs
What happens when I connect Google Calendar?

We do not store any of your calendar or Google account data with us. When you connect the Google Calendar, this web app syncs with the Calendar directly. In the Settings, you can choose if you wish to display only the events created here or all Google Calendar events. You can edit or delete an event ONLY from Google Calendar not from Hora Vedic Planner.

What is the difference between LST and LMT?

LST (Local Standard Time) follows your regional clock. LMT (Local Mean Time) adjusts explicitly for your precise geographic longitude offset from the timezone meridian, which alters boundary timings.

How does Hora Vedic Planner data differ from data on other softwares?

Hora Vedic Planner is a free to use tool and is being designed to remain free forever. Paid softwares use Swiss Ephemeris which requires licensing fees but Hora Vedic Planner uses open source astronomical libraries for our calculations which is accurate within sub-arcminute of Swiss Ephemeris for 100 year span.

What is the definition of sunrise and sunset used in Hora Vedic Planner?

For calculation purposes, sunrise and sunset tracks the moment the upper rim of the sun is tangent to an unobstructed horizon at sea level.
