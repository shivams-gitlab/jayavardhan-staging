Transits (Gocara)

Graha are continuously moving through the zodiac, and as they change signs, our experiences change with them. The Moon (Chandra) takes about 2.5 days to cross one sign, while mighty Saturn (Sani) takes about 2.5 years. In this course we learn to interpret the results of the transits of all graha.

The course is based on Muhurta Chintamani, Deva Keralam, Phaldeepika, Brihat Jataka, Prasna Marga and other texts.

Prerequisite: a basic understanding of the chart, including dasha.

Schedule

  1. Week 1 - Introduction and transits of the Moon
  2. Week 2 - Transits of Mars, Mercury and Venus
  3. Week 3 - Transits of Jupiter
  4. Week 4 - Transits of the Sun; Sankranti
  5. Week 5 - Transits of Rahu-Ketu
  6. Week 6 - Transits of Saturn, Sade-Sati, Kantaka Sani
  7. Week 7 - Other key techniques
