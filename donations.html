Process of Consultation

What happens between booking a reading and actually sitting down for one.

1. Submit Request

Submit the booking form with your birth details. You'll get a confirmation email within 1–2 days to confirm acceptance of your request.

2. Chart Preparation

If booking is accepted, your chart is prepared in advance using your exact date, time, and place of birth - this is why accurate birth details matter.

3. Live Session or Audio

We meet live (video or phone) for readings and consultations, or you'll receive a recorded audio walkthrough for the 10-min Audio option.

4. Payment / Donation

Donation-based sessions are settled afterward, at whatever amount felt right to you. Paid consultations are settled at booking - see below.

*Some requests are NOT accepted due to certain reasons. In such cases, you will be asked to revisit after sometime, preferably after 40 days.

Cancellation, Rescheduling, Refunds Policy

Please read before booking a donation-based or paid session.

If you wish to reschedule or cancel the appointment (except 10-min Audio), please do let me know 72 hours ~ 3 days prior to our meeting. I spend considerable time on preparing for each consultation, your timely communication will help me better manage my time.

If you cancel twice for the same consultation request, you would be considerably deprioritized for all your future requests.

There is no cancellation for "10-min Audio Consultation" or "Muhurta Consultation" and no refunds will be provided.

No Refunds will be provided for any donation-based consultation as payments are based on your satisfaction.
