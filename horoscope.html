Monthly Horoscope

Not your typical Gregorian month but the month personal to you, as the Moon transits your nakshatra we look ahead into the lunar month — here's the theme to expect.

1. Ashwini Ketu

[Day 1–2 — update with real transit dates]

Favors fast starts — a new routine, a first attempt, a quick decision that pays off. Physical energy runs high, so use it before it turns restless.

2. Bharani Venus

[Day 2–3 — update with real transit dates]

A period of creative intensity and letting go of something that's run its course. What feels like an ending is really clearing space for the next thing.

3. Krittika Sun

[Day 3–4 — update with real transit dates]

Sharp clarity arrives — you'll see exactly what needs cutting away. Good window for honest conversations and decisive action, less so for patience.

4. Rohini Moon

[Day 4–5 — update with real transit dates]

Growth-oriented and sensory — money, comfort, and relationships all get easier to build. A good stretch for anything that needs steady nurturing.

5. Mrigashira Mars

[Day 5–6 — update with real transit dates]

Restless curiosity takes over; you'll want to search, explore, or start researching something new. Follow it, but avoid committing until the picture is clearer.

6. Ardra Rahu

[Day 6–7 — update with real transit dates]

Expect a bit of upheaval — old structures loosen so something more honest can take their place. Uncomfortable in the moment, clarifying in hindsight.

7. Punarvasu Jupiter

[Day 7–8 — update with real transit dates]

A return or renewal theme — reconnecting with a person, place, or plan you'd set aside. Optimism is well-supported this period.

8. Pushya Saturn

[Day 8–9 — update with real transit dates]

One of the steadier windows — good for routines, commitments, and anything that needs consistency rather than excitement.

9. Ashlesha Mercury

[Day 9–10 — update with real transit dates]

Intuition runs sharp; trust what you're sensing beneath the surface of a situation. Not the best window for taking things at face value.

10. Magha Ketu

[Day 10–11 — update with real transit dates]

Themes of legacy, authority, and ancestry surface. A good period for honoring where you come from while deciding what to carry forward.

11. Purva Phalguni Venus

[Day 11–12 — update with real transit dates]

Leans toward rest, pleasure, and creative play. Give yourself permission to enjoy something without justifying it.

12. Uttara Phalguni Sun

[Day 12–13 — update with real transit dates]

Favors partnership and follow-through — agreements made now tend to hold. Good window for formalizing something you've been circling.

13. Hasta Moon

[Day 13–14 — update with real transit dates]

Skilled, hands-on energy — a strong period for craft, precision work, or finishing something that needs a careful touch.

14. Chitra Mars

[Day 14–15 — update with real transit dates]

Design and presentation matter more than usual. Good window for anything visual, structural, or image-related.

15. Swati Rahu

[Day 15–16 — update with real transit dates]

Independence and flexibility are favored — plans that require bending with circumstances go more smoothly than rigid ones.

16. Vishakha Jupiter

[Day 16–17 — update with real transit dates]

Determined, goal-driven energy. Good for pushing a long-term plan forward, less suited to starting something entirely new.

17. Anuradha Saturn

[Day 17–18 — update with real transit dates]

Friendship, loyalty, and devotion take center stage. A good period for deepening existing bonds rather than forming new ones.

18. Jyeshtha Mercury

[Day 18–19 — update with real transit dates]

Leadership and protection themes — you may find yourself stepping up for someone or something. Handle authority carefully this period.

19. Mula Ketu

[Day 19–20 — update with real transit dates]

An uprooting, get-to-the-bottom-of-it energy. Good for investigating and closing loops, not for starting anything fragile.

20. Purva Ashadha Venus

[Day 20–21 — update with real transit dates]

Early-stage confidence and momentum — good for the opening move of something ambitious, even before all details are settled.

21. Uttara Ashadha Sun

[Day 21–22 — update with real transit dates]

Favors lasting achievement over quick wins. What you build carefully now tends to hold up well over time.

22. Shravana Moon

[Day 22–23 — update with real transit dates]

A listening, learning period — conversations, study, and absorbing new information all go well. Less suited to broadcasting your own opinions.

23. Dhanishta Mars

[Day 23–24 — update with real transit dates]

Rhythmic, prosperous energy tied to music, groups, and material gain. Good window for collaborative projects with momentum.

24. Shatabhisha Rahu

[Day 24–25 — update with real transit dates]

Healing and hidden-truth themes — good for research, therapy, or quietly working through something private.

25. Purva Bhadrapada Jupiter

[Day 25–26 — update with real transit dates]

Intense, transformative energy. Big realizations are more likely now than small, comfortable ones.

26. Uttara Bhadrapada Saturn

[Day 26–27 — update with real transit dates]

A deep, slow-moving period well suited to reflection, rest, or spiritual practice rather than fast action.

27. Revati Mercury

[Day 27–28 — update with real transit dates]

A completion and transition theme — wrapping up one chapter gently before the next one properly begins.
